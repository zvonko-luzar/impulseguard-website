What is FILLMANY?

FILLMANY is an independent shopping decision-support project designed to help consumers compare product offers and make more informed purchasing decisions.

Our Vision

We aim to develop tools that help shoppers identify comparable offers, understand total purchase costs, and avoid unnecessary impulse purchases.

Planned Features

  • Product and price comparison
  • Clear separation of exact matches and alternatives
  • Shipping-inclusive price information when verified
  • Optional purchase reflection reminders
  • Transparent merchant and affiliate relationships

Independence and Transparency

Our goal is to prioritize reliable product information and transparent comparisons. Future affiliate partnerships may support the service. Any commercial relationships will be disclosed appropriately.

Project Status

FILLMANY is currently under development. Public shopping comparison functionality is not yet available. This website provides information about the planned service.
